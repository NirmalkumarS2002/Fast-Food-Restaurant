Our journey so far

A few milestones that shaped how Stackly cooks and delivers today.

2018

The first counter

Started as a 200 sq. ft. street kitchen serving three burgers and one shake — cooked to order, every time.

2020

Our first full kitchen

Moved into a proper kitchen space, expanded the menu, and began focused hiring for our core cooking team.

2022

Online ordering launched

Brought Stackly online so people could order from home while we kept the same cook-fresh standard.

2026

18 cities and counting

Now delivering across 18 cities, still run by people who taste-test every new item before it goes on the menu.
